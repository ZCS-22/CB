Meet the Founder

Valli Arunagiri

Chennai Beats began with a simple belief: dance is for everyone. Founded by Valli Arunagiri, the academy brings the joy, culture, and rhythm of Indian and South Indian dance to the Seattle area — helping kids, teens, and adults find their confidence on and off the stage.
